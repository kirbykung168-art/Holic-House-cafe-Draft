— Our message

We're a garden cafe. The atmosphere is warm and friendly, the prices are friendly, and the coffee is made to order — tell us how you like it and we'll pull it that way.

The tea steeps in our own pot. The bakery case runs small and gets rebuilt every morning. The garden is shaded by the tree most of the day. Come sit a while.
